# MyBots

### Open infrastructure for robots that evolve.

MyBots is building modular robotic systems and the software, models, and interfaces that make them extensible.

---

### Research

Our work spans the stack required to connect simulation, perception, prediction, and physical machines.

* **Sensor Twins** — Models of real sensor behavior for simulation and validation
* **3D Occupancy & Flow** — LiDAR and radar representations of the environment
* **Motion Prediction** — Ego and agent forecasting
* **Vector Prediction** — Structured representations of dynamic scenes
* **Scene Understanding** — Multimodal world representation
* **End-to-End Driving** — Multimodal models for autonomous navigation
